How to Find a Townhouse With Garage or Parking

By Stanley Montfort

Discover your dream townhouse in New York City without breaking the bank on parking! We understand that parking in the city can be costly, but fear not. We’re here to guide you toward finding a townhouse with convenient on-site parking at an affordable price. Embrace the possibilities and start your real estate search today!

 

 

Where to Look for a townhouse with garage?

Knowing where to look will help you find an amazing place to call home, right in the city, with parking that will fit your budget. Are you going to be able to find a luxury apartment overlooking Central Park with a heated parking facility for under one million dollars? No, that is not going to happen, but there are some listings and some strategies where you can find something you will both love and be able to afford.

There are many neighborhoods in places like Harlem, Brooklyn and Queens that are fantastic places to live and have great homes with parking at a reasonable price. They may not be cheap, but they are reasonable.

Begin your search in the neighborhoods that tend to have detached townhouses. Often this type of construction has an alley in between the units that has a driveway, and sometimes even a garage.

Another strategy is to look in the outer neighborhoods, often these homes are much newer, having been built in the 1920s to 1960s. Many of these buildings were constructed with automobile parking in mind, unlike the older neighborhoods. However, there are townhouse pockets in Manhattan such as Strivers Row that are equipped with garages.

 

Benefits of Townhouse With Garage

Discover the endless possibilities of owning a car in the city. Picture yourself enjoying spontaneous weekend getaways, indulging in luxurious shopping sprees, and relishing in the unmatched freedom of having your own set of wheels. While public transportation may cover most of your needs, certain situations call for the indispensability of a car.

Having a garage proves immensely advantageous, even in areas with seemingly abundant street parking. Many city streets enforce alternate side parking during winter months for snow removal, but with on-site parking, you can easily avoid this hassle. Plus, a covered parking space ensures your car remains safe from the elements, saving you from potential damage and costly repairs in the long run.

Another added benefit is the ability to install charging ports in homes for electric cars, eliminating the need to find external charging stations. On top of that, you no longer have to worry about someone scratching or damaging your car while it’s parked. Properties with parking also tend to retain more value, giving you an even greater return on your investment.

That’s why purchasing a home with on-site parking or a garage is a smart choice for many city residents. And here’s the exciting part—affordable parking options are out there if you know where to look. Don’t miss out on the convenience, ease, security, and value that come with having your own house and own parking space.

Drawbacks of Owning a Townhouse with a Garage

Discover the value of having your own parking spot without breaking the bank. While parking can be costly, ranging in price from $50,000 to a million dollars depending on the location, the convenience it offers to city residents outweighs the expense. Keep in mind that with a townhouse and a garage, you may sacrifice some outdoor space, such as a garden. Additionally, maintenance issues like stuck garage doors or replacement may arise. However, the good news is that you don’t have to empty your pockets to find a townhouse with parking. By utilizing the right search strategy, you can find surprisingly affordable options. So, if having a dedicated parking space is essential to you, rest assured that there is a suitable townhouse out there that won’t strain your budget.

 

Top Budget-Friendly Townhouses With a Garage or Parking

 

 

235 West 138th Street

This description outlines a captivating townhouse located in the historic district of “Striver’s Row” in Harlem. The property boasts a range of original features and details, with amenities such as dark wood wainscoting, pocket doors, high ceilings, and mantels, which have been well-preserved. Notably, the “alleyway” that was initially intended for deliveries has been repurposed to provide parking access.

The current layout of the townhouse consists of one duplex apartment and four separate studio units. These units share common sitting, kitchen, and bathroom areas. This arrangement presents an attractive opportunity for a family looking to generate rental income.

 

218 West 139th Street

Don’t miss out on this unique opportunity to own one of the only eight corner townhouses on Striver’s Row. This stunning, 20-foot wide, two-family home with southern, northern and western exposures offers elegant features such as French doors, crown molding, six decorative fireplaces, 12-foot ceilings, a North-facing Juliet-balcony, and a full finished basement. With over 4800 square feet of living space on a 32ft × 100ft lot, there’s ample room to create your dream home. Built in 1893 by architects Bruce Price and Clarence S. Luce, the home boasts a striking yellow brick and white limestone with terracotta facade. The owner’s triplex on the parlor floor includes a sitting room, kitchen, dining room, and four bedrooms with three bathrooms and laundry on the next two floors. Additionally, there’s a spacious one-bedroom floor-through apartment on the garden floor. Park your car in the private garage or side lot.

For those interested in ny townhomes, this property offers a rare combination of historical charm and modern amenities.

 

263 West 139th Street

Don’t miss out on the only 19 ft wide townhouse with an open east view in Strivers Row. This unique dream home features a master bedroom with six windows and a windowed master bathroom. On the parlor floor, you’ll find a dining room, a stainless steel kitchen, and a living room with Juliet balcony doors. The third floor offers a large bedroom, family room, and a stunning 400 sq ft terrace with an amazing east view. The penthouse floor provides flexible space options. The Washer Dryer and Cellar Den are extra rooms in the owner’s triplex. Plus, there’s a private garden floor rental for additional income. Don’t miss the chance to be part of history at 263 West 139th Street.

 

 

2001 5th Ave

2001 5th Avenue is a exceptional and unique home that will leave you breathless. This meticulously renovated corner townhouse features an indoor pool and hot tub, accessible by stairs or elevator, a private two-car garage, and an adjoining apartment. Every inch exudes sophistication and tranquility. The cellar offers a sleek steam room, a separate sauna, and a fully equipped wet bar for relaxation. The pool and jacuzzi are a rare luxury in Manhattan living. On the next level, you’ll find a personal two-car garage, a private gym or bedroom, an additional bedroom or home office, and a spacious recreation room.

 

 

730 Saint Nicholas Avenue

Welcome to this iconic mansion on Saint Nicholas Avenue in Hamilton Heights! With its majestic charm and striking five-story limestone, this home boasts a dramatic turret and bay windows on every floor. Other notable features include a side driveway, a private garage, and over eight thousand square feet of glorious space. Inside, you’ll find soaring ceilings, grand rooms, and abundant windows throughout. This masterpiece is brimming with original details, including stained-glass windows, inlay parquet floors, and intricate molding. Explore the five floors of endless space, and enjoy the convenience of a garage, storage area, and a light-filled studio/office with skylights and garden views. This two-family home also offers rental potential on the garden floor. While some work is needed to complete the splendor, there’s no doubt that this is a one-of-a-kind residence. This home features eight bedrooms, four full baths, a formal dining room, and a charming garden.

 

 

All in all, finding a townhouse with a garage or parking is not as unattainable as it may seem. If you are looking for convenience, affordability and security to store your car, then finding a townhouse with a garage or parking can be the perfect option for you. Here we discussed the various benefits of owning a townhouse with these features and the drawbacks associated with them. Also, the listing information provided were examples of great listings that include garages and parking spaces.
