    Buying a home in New York City involves far more than calculating principal and interest. Co-op maintenance fees, condo common charges, property taxes, closing costs, and building assessments can significantly impact your true monthly housing expenses. This guide explains how a specialized NYC mortgage calculator helps buyers understand the full financial picture before making an offer.

    Whether you're considering a condo, co-op, townhouse, or investment property, understanding all ownership costs is essential for making confident decisions.
