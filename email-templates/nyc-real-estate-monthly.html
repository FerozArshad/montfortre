04 • SMARTER REAL ESTATE

The Rate Float-Down Strategy: How NYC Buyers Are Lowering Monthly Costs Today

Instead of waiting for Federal Reserve rate cuts that may take months to materialize, sophisticated buyers are using seller-paid rate buy-downs.

A 2-1 temporary buy-down lowers your interest rate by 2% in year one and 1% in year two, fully funded by the seller at closing. This preserves your liquidity during move-in while keeping your fixed long-term refinancing option open when market rates drop.
01Target multi-family townhomes with flexible C of O potential.
02Lock rate-drop float-downs with trusted NYC portfolio lenders.
03Run carrying-cost stress tests before making non-contingent offers.
